# Enrolment Quest — World Story (Neem Morning arc)

**Tone:** mythic-storybook pixel · soft Indian everyday · cute not corporate-cold  
**Through-line:** You leave an ordinary morning and walk your family into a *shielded home*.

## Framing myth

You are a newly enrolled employee. Benefits aren’t paperwork — they’re **regions on the road home**.  
Each level is a place you must cross. Each choice outfits you (and yours) with protection.  
The destination isn’t an office. It’s a courtyard where everyone you named can stand in the light.

## Emotional arc

1. **Belong** — gather your people  
2. **Protect** — put a health shield around them  
3. **Flourish** — optional care for everyday life  
4. **Endure** — a quiet promise if you’re gone  
5. **Arrive** — everyone under one roof, waving

## Art continuity (Neem Morning DNA)

- Soft Indian cues: neem / gulmohar, terracotta, marigold, verandah — never flags or religious caricature  
- Style C mythic storybook pixel (matches locked hero)  
- L1 is the “home key”; later levels recolour and re-theme the same world language  
- Completion echoes L1 cottage language but elevated — earned homecoming, not a new planet
