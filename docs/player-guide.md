# Player guide

A quick guide to Enrolment Quest — a game that helps you explore your company
benefits before you enrol for real.

## 1. What is this?

Enrolment Quest is a short platformer where you walk your character through
your benefits — building your family, comparing medical cover, picking
optional add-ons, and (for some companies) choosing life cover. **This is an
exploration/interest game, not your final enrolment.** Nothing you choose
here is submitted as your real elections — at the end, you're handed a link
and QR code to your company's actual enrolment portal, where you confirm
everything for real.

## 2. How to play

- **Move**: arrow keys / A-D. **Jump**: up / space / W.
- Walk into the glowing portals ("Focus Zones") along each level to open that
  level's choice screen. You can revisit and change your picks before moving
  on.
- The game has up to 4 levels, played in order. Not every company enables all
  4 — see below.

## 3. Level 1 — Family First

Build your household: add your spouse, children, and/or parents (up to the
limit your company's policy allows), and mark who you'd like covered. This
sets up who the rest of the game — and your real enrolment — will cover.

## 4. Level 2 — Medical Cover

Compare your company's medical plan tiers side by side — sum insured,
premium, room rent, co-pay, maternity cover, and more — and pick the one that
fits your family.

## 5. Level 3 — Wellbeing Bazaar

Browse optional add-ons (things like outpatient care, dental, vision,
fitness memberships, or health check-ups). Some are priced per person, some
cover your whole family in one purchase. Each card tells you who's eligible.

## 6. Level 4 — Term Life *(if available)*

Some companies also offer a fourth level: choosing a term life cover amount
and naming your nominees. If your company hasn't enabled this level, you
won't see it — you'll go straight from Level 3 to the finish.

## 7. Where enrolment actually happens

At the end of the run, you'll see your results and a QR code / link to your
company's real benefits portal. **Your in-game picks are for exploration
only** — head to that portal (or the link your HR team shared) to confirm
your actual benefit elections.

## 8. For HR / admin

If the branding, plans, or add-ons on screen don't look like your company's,
someone may have left the game on a different company profile (there's a
hidden admin "COMPANY" switcher on the title screen used for testing). Reach
out to engineering with a screenshot if this happens — it's a local device
setting, not something wrong with your policy data.
